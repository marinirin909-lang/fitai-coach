FAQ

Questions, answered.Soalan lazim.

Everything people usually ask before they start.

Is FitAI Coach free?

Yes, you can start free: a 7-day full-body plan, daily meals, progress tracking and 5 AI Coach messages a day. Premium (RM39 a month) unlocks plans up to 90 days, every workout split, the full exercise library and 100 AI messages a day.

Does it work in Bahasa Melayu?

Yes. The whole app switches between English and Bahasa Melayu, and the AI Coach replies in the language you write in.

Do I need a gym?

No. Choose no equipment, dumbbells and bands, or a full gym when you build your plan, and every exercise matches what you have.

Are the meals halal?

You choose your diet: halal, vegetarian, vegan or no restriction. Halal meals use halal-friendly ingredients; check certification where you buy or eat.

Is the AI Coach a doctor?

No. It gives general fitness and nutrition guidance and tells you to see a professional for pain, injury, pregnancy or medical conditions. Every reply can be reported if something looks wrong.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, any time, in Settings inside the app or on our account deletion page. Your profile, plans, logs and chat history are removed.

Which devices does it run on?

Android phones through Google Play, and any modern browser through the web app. An iPhone version is in preparation.

How is my data protected?

Data is encrypted in transit, stored with Google Firebase, and never sold. Read the privacy policy for the full list of services we use.

Can I use it without an account?

Yes. Guest mode keeps your profile, plan and logs on your device. You need an account for the AI Coach and to sync across devices.

What happens when my plan ends?

The Today screen tells you the plan is finished and offers to build the next one.

Can I change my diet or goal later?

Yes. Settings, Edit profile. Your targets and meals update straight away.

Does it work offline?

Your plan, meals and logs work offline once loaded. Sync and the AI Coach need a connection.

Is it suitable during pregnancy or with a medical condition?

Please speak to your doctor before starting any new training or diet. FitAI Coach provides general guidance for healthy adults.

Your plan is ready when you are.

Answer three questions. Get a plan built for your equipment, your level and your food. Free to start.
