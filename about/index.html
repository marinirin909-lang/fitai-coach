About

Fitness advice that fits Malaysian life.Dibina di Malaysia, untuk rakyat Malaysia.

Most fitness apps assume a Western diet, cool weather and a gym on every corner. We built FitAI Coach for nasi campur, 33-degree afternoons and a dumbbell in the corner of the bedroom.

Why we built it

FitAI Coach started with a simple frustration: good coaching is expensive, and free advice online rarely fits the food, weather and schedules people here actually live with.

So we combined a structured training engine, a meal library built from familiar Malaysian dishes and an AI coach that speaks both of our main languages, then made it free to start.

What we believe

  • Consistency beats perfection. A plan you follow beats a perfect plan you quit.
  • Local food can be healthy food. You don't need imported superfoods.
  • Honesty over hype. No miracle claims, no crash diets, and clear limits on what an app can do.
  • Your data is yours. No ads, no selling data, and deletion whenever you want.
