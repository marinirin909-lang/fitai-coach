Frequently Asked Questions

Is FitAI Coach suitable for beginners? ▼
Absolutely! FitAI Coach is designed for all fitness levels. Our AI adapts workouts based on your current fitness level and progressively increases intensity as you improve. We have beginner-friendly exercises with detailed instructions in the exercise library.
Are the nutrition plans halal-certified? ▼
All nutrition recommendations are 100% halal-compliant, focusing on halal-certified foods available in Malaysia and Southeast Asia. Our AI only suggests ingredients and meals that follow Islamic dietary guidelines.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? ▼
Yes, you can cancel anytime with no penalty. Plus, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with your results in the first month, we'll refund your subscription fee fully.
Is there a free trial for Premium? ▼
We don't offer a free trial, but every subscription comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee. If FitAI Coach doesn't meet your expectations, you'll get a full refund. Plus, you can explore our exercise library preview for free to see what's inside.
Does FitAI Coach work offline? ▼
FitAI Coach requires an internet connection for real-time AI coaching, downloading your personalized plans, and syncing progress data. Some exercise descriptions can be viewed offline once downloaded in the app.
Can I use it for multiple family members? ▼
The Starter plan is for individual use. The Elite plan includes a Family Plan feature that allows you to create customized coaching profiles for up to 5 family members, each with their own goals and progress tracking.
