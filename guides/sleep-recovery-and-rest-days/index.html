Sleep, recovery and why rest days matter

Muscles grow between workouts, not during them. How to recover well so training keeps paying off.

Training breaks your body down a little. Recovery is when it rebuilds stronger. Skip recovery and you'll stall, feel tired all the time and raise your risk of injury.

Sleep comes first

Adults generally need 7 to 9 hours. Short sleep makes hunger harder to control, lowers training performance and slows recovery. Keep a regular bedtime, dim screens an hour before, and keep the bedroom cool and dark.

Rest days aren't lazy days

On rest days, light movement helps: a 20 to 30 minute walk, stretching, or mobility work like cat-cow and hip flexor stretches. FitAI Coach schedules these for you on Premium plans.

Deload weeks

Every four to six weeks of hard training, take a lighter week with about half the sets. You'll often come back stronger. Longer FitAI Coach plans include these automatically.

Signs you need more recovery

  • Your usual weights feel unusually heavy for over a week
  • You're sleeping badly or feel restless
  • Soreness lasts more than three days
  • You've lost motivation to train
