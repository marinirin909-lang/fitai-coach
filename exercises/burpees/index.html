Cardio

BurpeesBurpees

Squat, jump back to a plank, return and jump. A full-body conditioning exercise that needs no equipment.

CardioChestCoreNo equipmentIntermediateFree

How to do it

Setup. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart.

  1. Squat and place your hands on the floor.
  2. Jump or step your feet back to a plank, then bring them back in.
  3. Stand and jump, reaching your arms up.

Most common mistake

Landing hard on your lower back as you drop. Keep the plank firm.

Coach's tip

Step back instead of jumping if your knees or wrists complain.

Sets and reps by level

LevelSetsRepsRest
Beginner3 sets5-8 reps60s
Intermediate3 sets10-12 reps60s
Advanced4 sets12-15 reps60s
Stop if you feel sharp or joint pain. Mild muscle burning is normal; pain is not.
