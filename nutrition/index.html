How portions work

One meal, scaled to you.

Each day's calories are split 25% breakfast, 35% lunch, 30% dinner and 10% snack. Each meal's portion is scaled between 0.6× and 1.8× to hit that share, so a 1,600 kcal plan and a 2,600 kcal plan eat the same food in different amounts.
