A 20-minute home workout with no equipment

A full-body circuit for a small room, a condo or a hotel. No jumping option included for quiet floors.

No gym, no time, no problem. This circuit works your whole body in 20 minutes and fits in the space of a yoga mat.

How it works

Do each exercise for 40 seconds, rest 20 seconds, then move to the next. Finish all five, rest one minute, and repeat for four rounds.

  1. Bodyweight squat
  2. Push-up (on your knees or hands raised if needed)
  3. Glute bridge
  4. Mountain climbers
  5. Plank

Quiet version for apartments

Swap mountain climbers for slow alternating reverse lunges, and skip any jumping. Your downstairs neighbours will thank you.

Make it harder over time

Add a round, shorten the rest to 15 seconds, or swap in harder versions: lunges instead of squats, pike push-ups instead of push-ups.
